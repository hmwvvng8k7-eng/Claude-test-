Questions

Before you book

Do I need to get permission to fly?

No, that is our job. We hold a CAA General Visual Line of Sight Certificate, check every site against restricted airspace and request permission from airfields or landowners where needed.

What happens if the weather is bad?

We do not fly in winds above 24 mph, in rain or in low cloud. If the forecast turns, we move your booking to the next clear slot at no charge.

How high and how close can you fly?

UK rules cap us at 120 metres (400 ft) above the ground. We keep a safe distance from uninvolved people and plan take-off points so neighbours are not overflown.

Can I use the images however I like?

Yes. Every package includes a perpetual commercial licence for print, web, listings and advertising. We only ask to show a few in our portfolio.
